## ✨ What LinkPilot AI Pro Does

LinkPilot AI Pro is an AI-powered LinkedIn assistant that runs right in your browser:

- 🎯 **Pro Job Match Analyzer** — Paste or auto-detect a job description and get a compatibility score against your profile, along with identified skill gaps.
- 💬 **Smart Comment Generator** — Auto-detects posts in your feed and generates thoughtful, professional comments.
- 🤝 **Connection Request Generator** — Personalized, human-sounding connection notes based on the recipient's role and shared context.
- ⭐ **AI Profile Enhancer** — AI-powered suggestions to improve your headline, About section, and Experience descriptions.
- 🔍 **One-Click Auto-Detect** — No more copy-pasting; LinkPilot reads posts, job descriptions, and profile details straight from the page.
- 🔒 **Privacy First** — Your profile data is stored locally in your browser. Only the text you explicitly submit is processed to generate AI responses.

### How It Works

1. Open the LinkPilot AI side panel on any LinkedIn page.
2. Choose your mode — Job Match, Comment, or Connect.
3. Click Auto-detect or paste text, then let the AI analyze it.
4. Use the results directly on LinkedIn.
